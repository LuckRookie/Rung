# Review Card

Read when a diff, structure, risk, evidence, or delivery needs judgment.

## Operate

- Compare outcome, design, diff and evidence within the declared scope.
- Check counterevidence before confirming material findings.
- Check ownership, contracts, dependencies, state, errors and facts. For fixes, check that the cause and affected sibling paths are addressed without leaving parallel policy or caller workarounds.
- Confirm tests protect behavior, contracts, invariants, and failures without incidental internals.
- Fix authorized ordinary findings; route material findings to their concern.

Use [Software Quality](software-quality.md) for quality trade-offs, [Technical Debt](technical-debt.md) for future burden, [Project Model](project-model.md) for drift, [Engineering Structure](engineering-structure.md) for structure, and [Architecture Assessment](architecture-assessment.md) for system judgment.

Primary Agent reviews; consider an independent reviewer for contracts, security/privacy, data, architecture, migration, Harness authority or user request.

Stop when findings are resolved, routed or accepted. Output findings and risks; optionally use `assets/review-result.template.md`. See [Execution Model](execution-model.md) and [Project Harness](project-harness.md).
