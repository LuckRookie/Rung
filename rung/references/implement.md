# Implement Card

Read while editing when scope, order, ownership, integration or fact synchronization needs attention.

## Operate

- The Primary Agent implements; workers receive bounded, non-overlapping ownership.
- Tie edits to the outcome. For fixes, repair the cause at its owner, including necessary abstraction or responsibility changes; remove superseded policy paths. A minimal diff alone does not establish a coherent fix.
- Preserve user-owned edits and applicable project instructions.
- Keep touched placement, names, flow, errors, resources and facts coherent. Verify enabling cleanup; report unrelated findings.
- Synchronize tests, configuration, generated sources, and durable fact owners.
- Check meaningful units; integrate worker output before final verification.

Read [Software Quality](software-quality.md) for quality trade-offs, [Engineering Structure](engineering-structure.md) for nonlocal structure, and [Technical Debt](technical-debt.md) for future obligations. Revisit Design when direction changes.

Stop when changes and facts are integrated. Report the diff, checks and findings.

Use [Change Planning](change-planning.md) for task order and block closure; [Execution Model](execution-model.md) for recovery or delegation.
